# Crystal refinement — 3.0.10

The supplied ZIP geometry, brand, typography, colored flags and interlocking wedding rings remain the basis of the design. The user selected baby footprints for birth: two filled mint/coral footprints now replace the sprout everywhere the birth icon is used.

Archive, document, calendar and work icons use cleaner filled silhouettes and lighter details at small sizes. The generator retains every original source SVG and rebuilds 64 unique symbols; no emojis or CSS recoloring filters are used.

`src/styles/crystal.css` adds translucent navy/light surfaces, restrained blue/mint/lilac background light, rounded controls and a floating sidebar. Blur is limited on long mobile content; reduced-transparency/high-contrast preferences and browsers without backdrop-filter receive opaque surfaces. Text, flags and icons remain fully opaque. Existing horizontal/vertical timeline interactions are unchanged.

Selected: transparency on navigation and outer cards, original palette, visible colored glyphs, compact controls.
Declined: transparent text, heavy animated distortion, stacked blur in mobile lists, invented national flags, automatic person merges, and identity percentages presented as certainty.
